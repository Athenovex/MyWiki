# 떼어내는 스티커 만드는 프롬프트

### **🔧** 사용 앱

- 힉스필드 (Hixfield)

- Image → Video 기능 사용

- 원본 이미지 유지 옵션 ON

🔽 바꾸고자 하는 사진과 함께  아래 프롬프트를 복사해서 붙여넣으세요!

```javascript

The character is slowly peeled away diagonally from the top-right toward the bottom-left. During the peeling motion, the sticker bends naturally like real paper, with subtle curvature and realistic physical movement.
As the character is completely removed, the background is seamlessly reconstructed and naturally revealed behind it, with no gaps, distortions, or visible edits.
The hand then lifts the peeled sticker toward the camera and holds it in view. The sticker must remain identical to the original character in size, proportions, colors, and shape. A clean white sticker border surrounds the entire character, emphasizing the paper sticker effect.
Allow only a slight natural wobble while the sticker is being held. No changes to the character design, facial features, clothing, or proportions.
End on a frozen frame with the hand holding the sticker toward the camera.
```
